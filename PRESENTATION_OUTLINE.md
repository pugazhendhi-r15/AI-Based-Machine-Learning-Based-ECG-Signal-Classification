# Final Presentation Outline (10 slides)

## Slide 1 — Title
AI-Based ECG Signal Classification Using Machine Learning
Student name, department, institution, guide.

## Slide 2 — Problem Statement
ECG signals contain patterns that can be classified using labelled examples. The project builds an educational classifier for selected heartbeat groups.

## Slide 3 — Objectives
- Load a public labelled dataset.
- Preprocess and segment ECG waveforms.
- Train and compare two ML models.
- Evaluate model performance.
- Show results in a dashboard.

## Slide 4 — ECG Basics
Explain ECG, P wave, QRS complex, T wave, and what a labelled heartbeat annotation means. Avoid making diagnostic claims.

## Slide 5 — Dataset
MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database from PhysioNet. Describe the selected records and the simplified N/S/V label mapping.

## Slide 6 — System Architecture
ECG dataset → filtering → segmentation → feature extraction → ML training → evaluation → dashboard.

## Slide 7 — Preprocessing and Features
Show before/after filter plot and explain the feature list: mean, standard deviation, minimum/maximum, median, quartiles, peak position, energy, and mean absolute difference.

## Slide 8 — Models and Evaluation
Compare Random Forest and SVM. Show actual accuracy, per-class precision/recall/F1, macro-F1, and confusion matrix generated by the program.

## Slide 9 — Dashboard and Demo
Show record selection, ECG waveform, example beat segment, predicted class, and evaluation outputs.

## Slide 10 — Conclusion and Limitations
Summarize what was built. Clearly state that the prototype is educational, not clinically validated, and the starter beat-level split may overestimate performance. Mention record-wise evaluation as future work.
